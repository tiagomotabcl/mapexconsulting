What you’re assessed on

Five controls, explained plainly.

Cyber Essentials checks five technical controls set by the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC). These are the gaps I find and fix before you apply.

Firewalls

Traffic between the internet and your network is filtered, on the office router and on every laptop that leaves the building.

Common gaps I fixDefault router passwords, open remote access ports, laptop firewalls switched off.

Secure configuration

Devices and software are set up to close the obvious ways in: unused accounts, default settings and software nobody needs.

Common gaps I fixOld admin accounts, auto-run left on, unused software still installed.

Security update management

Operating systems, apps and firmware are patched quickly, and anything out of support is replaced.

Common gaps I fixUnsupported Windows versions, routers and plugins nobody updates.

User access control

People only get the access their job needs, admin rights are separate, and multi-factor authentication protects cloud services.

Common gaps I fixEveryone as local admin, shared logins, cloud accounts without MFA.

Malware protection

Every device has active protection that stops malicious software before it runs.

Common gaps I fixAntivirus disabled or expired, no control over what staff can install.

Questions

Before you apply.

Do I qualify for the discount?
The fund is for UK micro, small and medium-sized businesses that haven’t held Cyber Essentials in the last four years. Places are first come, first served.
What does the fund pay for, and what doesn’t it cover?
It pays 75% of the certification fee charged by the scheme. Getting your systems ready to pass isn’t covered: that’s the work I do, for a fixed fee agreed after the readiness review.
When does it end?
March 2027, or earlier if the £2.4m of funding runs out first.
Does it cover Cyber Essentials Plus?
No. The fund is for a new Cyber Essentials certificate. Plus adds an independent technical test, and I can prepare you for that afterwards.
Is there anything else included?
Yes. Organisations with Cyber Essentials and a turnover under £20m get up to £25,000 of cyber liability insurance with the certificate.
How long will it take?
It depends on what the readiness review finds. You’ll have a timeline and a fixed fee from me before any work starts.
